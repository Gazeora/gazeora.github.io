Get Gazeora

Free and open source. Install Gazeora on your Windows 10 or 11 PC, then get the app on your Android phone to watch your games on it.

x64

For 64-bit games

Most games from the last decade. Needs 64-bit Windows. Installs to Program Files\Gazeora.

x86

For 32-bit games

Every Direct3D 8 game and many older Direct3D 9 and OpenGL ones. Installs to Program Files (x86)\Gazeora.

APK

For your phone

The Gazeora viewer for Android 10 and newer. It finds your PC by itself and plays the stream flat or in a VR viewer.

Not sure which Windows installer? Start with x64. When you launch a game the other one captures, Gazeora tells you so. The two install side by side.

Installing

After you download

Let the stream through

The installer adds a Windows Firewall rule for private networks. If Windows calls your network public, devices can't connect until you make it private in Windows' network settings.

Upgrading

Run a newer MSI over the old one. Your game library, settings and USB driver stay. Uninstalling from Settings → Apps asks whether to delete your library and settings.

On your phone

Open the APK on the phone. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install apps, then open Gazeora on the same Wi-Fi as your PC.

Silent installs

msiexec /i Gazeora-1.0.0-x64.msi /qn
